# Audit of the 18 no_final_timing_admissible_time_plan cases

Reference finding: for all 18, gross kinematic reach and the joint-velocity
temporal bound are satisfied at many leads, with typical margins of 3 to 5 s.

Classification against the options given:
- A (no physically/path feasible solution exists): not supported
- B (physically feasible but no temporal solution): NOT supported at the gross
  velocity-bound level
- C (a temporal solution exists outside TRIAD's finite bank): consistent with
  the evidence but NOT established, because the reference ignores grasp,
  corridor and collision feasibility
- D (unresolved by the reference method): THIS IS THE CORRECT CLASSIFICATION
  for all 18

CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER STATEMENT. In the previous report I wrote that in
these cases "the arm cannot reach those events within any lead in the bank".
The independent analysis does not support that, and A7 explicitly warned
against it. What is established is only that all 18 produced complete plans
(3 to 356) whose predicted presentation durations left zero plans satisfying
the admission rule at receipt. Whether a solution exists outside TRIAD's finite
grasp/route bank is unresolved.
